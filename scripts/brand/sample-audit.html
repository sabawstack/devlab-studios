2. Where it stalls, and who owns each handoff

  1. Intake has no owner. Messenger and email inquiries wait until someone happens to check.
  2. The spreadsheet is the record, but it is filled in late. A callback depends on it.
  3. No follow-up step exists. An inquiry without a booking is never revisited.

3. Data and integrations

Four inbound channels, one spreadsheet, one clinic system with a booking API. No integration today; every transfer is manual. Data shown in this sample is synthetic.

4. Recommended system, drawn

Every inquiry is saved once, gets a named owner and a due time, and stays visible until it is booked or closed with a reason. Bookings go to the clinic system through its API; a failed handoff is shown to a person, not dropped.

5. Roadmap, in priority order

  1. One intake record for all four channels, with an owner. Phase 1
  2. A follow-up rule: no booking after two working days goes back to the owner. Phase 1
  3. Booking handoff to the clinic system, with failures visible. Phase 2
  4. Weekly report: inquiries, bookings, and what is waiting. Phase 3
