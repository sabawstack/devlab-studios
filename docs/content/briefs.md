# Article briefs (audit SEO-08)

Six articles the owner could write next, each tied to a solution line and a
problem tag (`src/config/problemTags.js`), written for the owner audience. These
are outlines only: the arguments, examples and numbers must come from real
engagements, not be invented.

| # | Working title | Problem tag | Solution page it supports | The question it answers for a buyer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who owns this inquiry? Fixing follow-up before it is a tool problem | `follow-up` | Lead intake and follow-up | Why do good leads go cold even though we "have a CRM"? |
| 2 | One inquiry, four inboxes: mapping where your leads actually land | `lead-intake` | Lead intake and follow-up | Where are inquiries arriving today, and which ones are we missing? |
| 3 | The monthly report nobody trusts | `reporting` | Custom software and operations | Why do our numbers disagree between tools, and what fixes that for good? |
| 4 | Handoffs: where automated work should stop and a person should start | `handoffs` | Workflow and AI automation | Which steps should stay human, and how do we make the handover reliable? |
| 5 | What a failed delivery should look like in your admin | `silent-failures` | Custom software and operations | How would we even know a message or sync failed last Tuesday? |
| 6 | Before you buy another app: auditing the workflow you already have | `choosing-tools` | Workflow Systems Audit | Should we fix the process or replace the software? |

For each: one real (anonymised) situation, the system drawn as a figure from the
scene library, what changed, and a closing link to the related solution page and
the audit. Tag the article and set its audience in the admin when publishing.
